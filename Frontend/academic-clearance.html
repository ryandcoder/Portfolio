Overview

Traditional school clearance requires students to carry physical forms across multiple offices, leading to long queues, lost paperwork, and unclear approval statuses. Academic Clearance moves that entire workflow online.

Built for clarity and speed, the system gives students a single dashboard to track requirements while providing faculty, office signatories, and administrators an organized interface to review, flag, or approve records.

What it does

  • Role-based access

    Separate views and permissions powered by Firebase Authentication for department signatories and administrators.

  • Real-time clearance tracking

    Students can view pending, flagged, and cleared statuses across every required office in one place.

  • Department sign-off queue

    Signatories can filter student records, add deficiency remarks, and approve clearances efficiently.

  • Requirement & deficiency notes

    Clear feedback explains exactly what a student needs to resolve before an office signs off.

  • Centralized student records & files

    Manages student lists, departments, clearance progress, and supporting documents via Firebase Storage.

  • Paperless audit trail

    Eliminates misplaced clearance cards by recording approval states and updates digitally.

Design direction

  • Status-first hierarchy. Clearance states (cleared, pending, deficient) are immediately readable without digging through menus.
  • Minimalist, distraction-free layout. Styled with Tailwind CSS, high-contrast typography, and clean tables to keep dense administrative data easy to scan.
  • Task-focused workflows. Built with React components to minimize clicks for signatories processing multiple student requests.
  • Responsive across devices. Accessible on both desktop workstations in offices and mobile phones for students checking updates.

  • Translating a multi-step real-world administrative workflow into a clear, predictable digital state machine.
  • Designing responsive, accessible interfaces with Tailwind CSS capable of displaying tabular records and status indicators without visual clutter.
